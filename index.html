Garden ToDo

What to do in your garden, and when.

Garden ToDo is an app for gardeners. It's a simple way to keep track of what needs doing in your garden – month by month, plant by plant.

A simple garden to do list, tailored to your garden

Gardening is easier when you have a clear sense of what needs doing and when. But in real life, that can be surprisingly hard to keep track of – especially when different plants need different jobs at different times.

Garden ToDo is being built to make seasonal garden care feel simpler. Instead of relying on memory, guesswork, or a generic gardening calendar, the aim is to give gardeners a more useful garden to do list for their own space.

How Garden ToDo works

The idea is simple:

  • Add your location – because timing varies depending on local climate and season.
  • Add your plants – because different plants need different care.
  • See what needs doing – with garden tasks tailored to your garden.

From pruning and sowing to dividing, protecting and cutting back, Garden ToDo is designed to help you keep track of seasonal gardening jobs throughout the year.

What it can help with

  • Knowing what to do in the garden this month
  • Keeping track of seasonal garden tasks
  • Understanding when to prune common plants
  • Remembering when to plant, divide or protect
  • Building confidence as your garden grows

Made for gardeners

Garden ToDo is for people who enjoy gardening but don't always want to keep everything in their head. Whether you grow flowers, shrubs, herbs, vegetables, or a bit of everything, the goal is to make garden care feel clearer and easier to stay on top of.

FAQ

What is Garden ToDo?

Will it work for different locations?
